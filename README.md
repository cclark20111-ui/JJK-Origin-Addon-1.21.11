# JJK-Origin-Addon-1.21.11
A jujustu kaisen based mc origins datapack
JJK Addon: Techniques and Abilities Guide
Every origin in the JJkAddon datapack for Minecraft 1.21.11 (Origins Legacy), with all abilities, passives, costs and drawbacks. Updated October 25, 2026.
How the Pack Works
Cursed Energy meter
• Every origin except Heavenly Restriction and Puppet Manipulation shares one Cursed Energy meter: 100 max, starts full, and refills at about 2 per second (roughly 50 seconds from empty to full).
• Every active ability spends energy. If you don’t have enough, a red Not enough Cursed Energy message appears and nothing happens. Each ability also has a short one-second lockout so holding the key can’t drain you.
• Many targeted abilities only spend energy if they land, so a miss is free. The exceptions are noted per ability.
• Special cases: Higuruma’s Confiscation can lock another player’s regeneration for 10 seconds, Geto regenerates 50% faster, and Hakari’s Jackpot makes energy infinite for 12 seconds.
Keys
• Primary is the Origins ability key (default G). Secondary is unbound by default, so set it in Controls.
• Third abilities (rituals) use sneak: hold sneak and press your primary key, then keep sneaking for 5 seconds while the Ritual bar fills. Letting go of sneak cancels it for free. A ritual origin’s normal primary ability only works when you are not sneaking.
Targeting and aim assist
• Aimed abilities (Switch, Hex Nail, Projection Cut, Manji Kick and others) first try an exact raycast, then a widening aim-assist tube along your line of sight, so near-misses still connect.
• Nearest-target abilities (Divine Flame, Supernova, Mahoraga) pick the nearest player first, then fall back to the nearest mob.
• Almost every attack works on both players and mobs.
Damage types
• Magic damage (most attacks) ignores armor but is reduced by Resistance, Protection enchantments and Absorption.
• Black Flash damage is true damage: it ignores armor, Resistance, enchantments, shields and invulnerability frames.
• Technique damage (Amber’s bonuses, Hammer and Nails) ignores armor, shields and invulnerability frames, but not Resistance.
• Sacrifice damage (Mahoraga’s price) ignores Totems of Undying but is reduced by armor, Protection and Resistance.
Summons and teams
Summoned creatures (Megumi’s dogs, rabbits and Warden, Geto’s spirits, Puppet Manipulation’s zombies) join a personal team made for the caster so they never attack their summoner. If the caster is already on another scoreboard team, the summons may treat them as an enemy.

The Origins
Cursed Speech
You speak in a language older than words. Crowd control with a poison price.
• Freeze (primary, 45): locks every living thing within 5 blocks in place for 3 seconds. They can’t move, attack or jump.
• Blast Away (secondary, 50): launches every living thing within 5 blocks high into the sky. Launched targets take no fall damage for 30 seconds, without needing Slow Falling.
• Cursed Vigor (passive): permanent Speed II.
• Drawback: both abilities leave you Poisoned for 10 seconds. A Honey Bottle cures it at once.
Heavenly Restriction
No cursed energy, so no meter and no energy costs. A body honed beyond human limits, built on speed and stealth, with its abilities limited by cooldowns instead.
• Flash Step (primary, free, 8 second cooldown): moves you up to 10 blocks in whatever direction you’re looking, stopping early if a wall is in the way.
• Phantom Hunter (secondary, free, 2 minute cooldown): true invisibility for 20 seconds, with your armor hidden too, plus Strength II and Speed II for the first 10 seconds. The extra 10 seconds of invisibility are there for hit and run.
• Immovable Frame (passive): reduced knockback.
• Unbreakable Body (passive): no fall damage.
Six Eyes + Limitless
Nothing crosses the space between you and your target without permission.
• Reversal (primary, 30): repels every entity within 8 blocks with overwhelming force, including players, mobs and projectiles.
• Amplification (secondary, 30): draws the entity in your line of sight sharply toward you.
• Limitless Presence (passive): every player within 100 blocks glows. The outline is visible to everyone, not only to you.
• Drawback: both abilities leave you Blinded for 10 seconds.
Shrine
Divine, destructive flame, and the earth tearing open at your command.
• Divine Flame (primary, 35): rains 8 fireballs plus 2 small fireballs on the nearest player (or nearest mob within 30 blocks), setting them ablaze and dealing about 2 extra hearts of fire damage and 3 hearts of magic damage.
• Dismantle (secondary, 35): tears a wide field of evoker fangs out of the ground in front of you, erupting in waves up to about 8 blocks away. You get brief Resistance V so the fangs don’t hurt you.
• Flameborn Body (passive): immune to fire and lava.
• Drawback: both abilities leave you with Slowness II for 3 seconds.
Blood Manipulation
Your own blood is your weapon, and it feeds you back.
• Supernova (primary, 60): plants four blood bombs around the nearest player (or mob). After about a 1.5 second fuse they detonate into radius-3 pools of Harming II that linger for around 6 seconds.
• Piercing Blood (secondary, 45): fires a lance in a straight line (up to 40 blocks). The first entity hit takes Harming I and Wither I for 4 seconds.
• Blood Ward (passive): immune to Poison and Wither.
• Blood Drain (passive): every hit you land heals you 2 hearts, with a 6 second recharge.
• Drawback: both abilities leave you Weakened for 5 seconds.
Boogie Woogie
You bend position itself, loudly.
• Switch (primary): swaps places with any entity you’re looking at, up to 30 blocks away, with aim assist. A clean swap costs 5 energy, a whiff costs 20.
• Object Teleport (secondary, 20): throws an ender pearl in the direction you’re looking.
• Honed Reflexes (passive): permanent Strength I.
• Drawback: a teleport chime is heard by everyone nearby whenever you use either ability.
Mythical Beast Amber
A storm in human form: speed that ends in lightning, then a heavy price.
• Speed Blitz (primary, 90): Speed VI for 6 seconds. Anything you strike is hit by lightning and takes 2 extra hearts of damage (at most once every 1.5 seconds). A trail of sparks follows you.
• Lightning Blast (secondary, 55): fires a wind charge forward. The first living thing in its path is struck by lightning and takes 5 extra hearts of damage.
• Beast’s Swiftness (passive): permanent Speed I.
• Drawback: when Speed Blitz ends you suffer Wither II for 8 seconds.
Idle Death Gamble (Hakari)
A slot machine bolted to your soul.
• Private Pure Love Train (primary, 50): spin the reels, with 5 seconds between spins.
    ◦ Jackpot (10%): energy is restored and becomes infinite for 12 seconds, with Regeneration II, Resistance I and Strength I.
    ◦ Small win (38%): Regeneration II, Speed II and 25 energy back.
    ◦ Bust (52%): a few seconds of Slowness and 10 energy back.
• Rough Energy (secondary, 35): hurls a ball of energy that detonates on the first entity in your line of sight (up to 30 blocks), dealing 4 hearts to everything within 3 blocks.
• Unending Stamina (passive): permanent Resistance I.
• Drawback: a Jackpot makes you glow for everyone to see, and your luck runs out for 90 seconds afterward so you cannot spin again.
Deadly Sentencing (Higuruma)
Every fight is a trial.
• Confiscation (primary, 30): targets the first living thing in your line of sight (25 blocks). A meter user loses 40 cursed energy and can’t regenerate it for 10 seconds, and you store 40 Verdict. Anything without a meter gets Weakness II for 10 seconds and gives you 25 Verdict.
• Executioner’s Sword (secondary, 40): summons a blade on the first living thing in view (20 blocks). Needs at least 25 Verdict. Damage by Verdict: 4 hearts at 25+, 7 hearts at 50+, 11 hearts at 80+. Only spends energy and Verdict if it lands.
• Judgeman’s Composure (passive): Resistance I while your energy is full.
• Verdict bar: stored Verdict fades at about 0.5 points per second, so strike while it’s fresh.
Straw Doll (Nobara)
Hammer, nails and a straw doll.
• Hex Nail (primary, 30): marks the first living thing in view (20 blocks, with aim assist) as hexed. The mark glows for 10 seconds. You can hex several targets.
• Resonance (secondary, 55): every hexed target within 25 blocks, in sight or not, takes 3 hearts and loses its hex.
• Hammer and Nails (passive): your melee hits on a hexed target deal 1 extra heart.
• Drawback: Resonance costs you 2 hearts however many targets it hits, and it will not fire at 3 hearts or less.
Idle Transfiguration (Mahito)
You reshape souls like clay.
• Touch (primary, 30, 8 second cooldown): touches the first living thing within 5 blocks and inflicts one random affliction: Nausea (6 s), Blindness (3 s), Slowness III (3 s), Poison II (4 s), Weakness II (6 s) or Wither II (3 s).
• Self-Embodiment of Perfection (secondary, 50): heals 6 hearts and clears every status effect on you, good or bad.
• Pliant Body (passive): no fall damage.
• Flesh Remolding (passive): a short burst of Regeneration I every 10 seconds.
• Drawback: 15% of Touches backfire on you with Nausea, Slowness II or Weakness for 5 seconds.
Ratio Technique (Nanami)
A professional who clocks out at five.
• 7:3 Strike (primary, 40, 6 second cooldown): a guaranteed 2.5 hearts of damage that ignores armor, on the first living thing within 5 blocks. Only costs energy if it lands.
• Overtime (secondary, 50): Strength I and Speed II for 8 seconds.
• Professional Composure (passive): permanent Resistance I.
• Drawback: when Overtime ends you are spent, with Slowness II and Weakness II for 8 seconds.
Ten Shadows (Megumi)
Your shadow is a menagerie.
• Divine Dogs (primary, 45, while not sneaking): two tamed wolves with Speed I fight for you for 20 seconds. Summoning again replaces them.
• Rabbit Escape (secondary, 35, 30 second cooldown): releases ten weakened killer rabbits for 4 seconds (each bites for about 2 hearts, and they drop nothing). Everything within 6 blocks is blinded for 3 seconds, and you turn invisible with Speed II for 8 seconds.
• Divine General Mahoraga (ritual, 100): hold sneak and press primary, then keep sneaking for 5 seconds. Summons a Warden with Speed III and Strength II that hunts your nearest enemy and disappears when its target dies or after 20 seconds.
• Shadow Walker (passive): Speed I and invisibility in low light.
• Drawback: Mahoraga costs your entire meter and inflicts 30 hearts of damage on you. Armor, Protection and Resistance reduce it, but a Totem of Undying does not save you. If no target is in range when the ritual finishes, nothing is spent. Creative mode does not protect you from the damage.
Projection Sorcery (Naoya)
Twenty-four frames per second, and you expect everyone to keep up.
• 24 Frames (primary, 45): Speed IV and Haste IV for 6 seconds, with afterimage particles. Your melee hits freeze the target (no moving or jumping) for 1 second, at most once every 5 seconds.
• Projection Cut (secondary, 45, 6 second cooldown): blinks you behind the first living thing in view (20 blocks, with aim assist), dealing 2 hearts and freezing it for 1 second.
• Zenin Reflexes (passive): permanent Speed I.
• Drawback: if you are hit while 24 Frames is active, you freeze for 1 second too (at most once every 3 seconds).
Black Flash (Yuji)
Your fists outrun your curse.
• Black Flash (passive): each bare-handed punch has an 8% chance to land a Black Flash of true damage (1.5 hearts). Landing one puts you in the zone, and each following flash is likelier but weaker: 20% for 1.25 hearts, 32% for 1 heart, 44% for 0.75 hearts, until the fifth lands at 56% for 1.5 hearts and the zone ends. The zone fades after 10 seconds without a flash. A bar on your HUD shows your streak.
• Divergent Fist (primary, 30): hits the first living thing within 5 blocks for 3 hearts, then a delayed echo hits for 4 hearts one second later. Only costs energy if it lands.
• Manji Kick (secondary, 35): launches you at the first living thing in view (12 blocks, with aim assist) for 4 hearts and Slowness III for 2 seconds. Only costs energy if it lands.
• Vessel’s Body (passive): permanent Strength I and Haste I.
• Fists Only (passive): swords can’t be wielded. Any sword in your main hand is dropped at once, with a short pickup delay.
• Drawbacks: Black Flash only works with an empty main hand. Spamming punches faster than about two a second blocks any flash and gives you Weakness until you slow down.
Cursed Spirit Manipulation (Geto)
You command every curse you have swallowed.
• Cursed Spirit Release (primary, 45): releases three cursed spirits (phantoms) that dive at every enemy player for 12 seconds. They never attack you or your allies. Summoning again replaces them.
• Maximum: Uzumaki (secondary, 60): a great creature materializes ahead of you and, after a 1 second wind-up, fires a spiralling beam along your line of sight that hits everything in its path for 4 hearts.
• Spirit Stockpile (passive): your cursed energy recovers 50% faster, about 3 per second.
• Drawback: Maximum: Uzumaki leaves you Nauseous for 6 seconds.
Puppet Manipulation
You fight with an army of puppets and almost nothing else.
• Puppet Manipulation (primary, free, 3 second delay between presses): each press raises a zombie puppet with Speed II and Resistance II that fights for you for 60 seconds, up to 10 at a time. Puppets never attack you.
• Puppeteer’s Frailty (passive): permanent Weakness IV, so your own attacks do almost nothing.
• Fragile Body (passive): you take 50% more damage from every source.
• Drawback: there is no Cursed Energy meter. The price is your own weakness and fragility. Puppets can spawn with random zombie gear.
Notes for the Pack Owner
• After changing the datapack, fully restart the world or server. Custom damage types and functions are not picked up by a plain reload.
• If someone gets stuck unable to jump after a freeze, fix them with /attribute <player> minecraft:jump_strength modifier remove cursed_origins:frozen_20 (or frozen_40, frozen_60). Blast Away’s fall-damage protection removes itself after 30 seconds.
• A Warden spawned by a command normally digs back into the ground. Mahoraga’s Warden is given a no-dig timer and is provoked into hunting its target. If it still despawns early, note whether the target was a player or a mob.
• Phantom Hunter’s true invisibility relies on Origins’ own invisibility power to hide armor. If armor still shows, report it.
• Jogo (Disaster Flames) is the one planned technique that has not been built.
• Players who already chose an origin before an update may need /origin set @s origins:origin cursed_origins:<id> to pick up new powers.
